Security Orchestration, Automation & Response — built to close the gap between alert and action.

FLAGSHIP PROJECT

An AI-powered SOAR platform for faster, more consistent incident response

Security teams are often buried in alerts with no consistent way to triage them. Yemen SOAR integrates directly with Wazuh SIEM to ingest detections, then runs them through automated playbooks — reducing response time and cutting down on manual, repetitive analyst work.

SOLUTION
